 SENSOR NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------

  • forward_track is the dead-ahead (0°) radar beam.
    It measures the distance to the track boundary ahead of the
    car's current nose direction. It is NOT lateral clearance.

  • A reading of 200 m means 'no boundary detected within range',
    not that a wall is exactly 200 m away.

  • A single short sample may be sensor noise. Zones with fewer
    than 5 samples should be treated with caution.

  • min_valid_track is the minimum reading across all 19 angled
    beams. It can indicate proximity to track edges or barriers
    that the forward beam alone would not detect.

  • The 40 m / 150 m thresholds are starting points.
    Use --corner-threshold and --straight-threshold to refine them
    once you have verified the circuit geometry.

  • Distance from start and zone lengths are derived from the
    distFromStart sensor and reflect where the car was, not the
    true geometric length of the corner.
